## #tag

Writing #showcase #v03 #demo in the body auto-links to `/_perspectives_/tags#tag`.

> [!note] Tag character rules
> Obsidian disallows `.` `,` `?` etc. in tags. For a version number with a `.` (like `v0.3`), write it as `v03` / `v0-3` / `v-0-3`, or just use frontmatter `tags: [v0.3]` (frontmatter fields have no such restriction).
